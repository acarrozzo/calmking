How to play

  • Pick a pieceClick or tap it. Tab cycles through them all, Shift+Tab back.
  • Move itClick a lit tile, drag toward it, or press ←↑↓→
  • PushWalk a piece into another and it shoves it along.
  • UndoZ — takes back a whole turn, sliding and all.
  • RestartR
  • LevelsEsc

Any piece can be moved, not only the King. Every one of them is a counterweight, and the far ones pull hardest.
